WHAT AM I LOOKING AT?

Protein design today means stitching together a dozen-plus tools — backbone generators, sequence designers, structure predictors, dockers, developability screens. This map, inspired by London Underground treats that ecosystem like a subway system: stations are tools, colored lines are common workflows, and tracks are the handoffs between them.

Every track is labeled with the metric that tells you whether to proceed — cross a threshold, move to the next station. Regular stations belong to a single workflow. Double-ringed stations are transfer stations — tools shared across multiple workflows.

HOW TO USE IT

Pick a line up top to isolate a workflow, or click any station or track to see full inputs, outputs, and thresholds. Use search (top right) to jump straight to a station, ↺ to reset the view, and ↓ to export what you're currently looking at as a PNG.

The visual language is borrowed from Harry Beck's 1933 London Underground map.
